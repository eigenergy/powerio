# PowerIO 0.11.9 release notes

PowerIO 0.11.9 is a small PSS/E release. A two winding transformer read from
PSS/E now records what its reader folds into the branch, so a consumer that
moves the tap can separate the quantities again:

- the factor its magnetizing admittance was referred by,
  `psse_magnetizing_referral`;
- winding 2's bus-base ratio, `psse_winding2_ratio`.

`Transformer3W::impedance_correction_control_winding` is now public. The
three winding star expansion now carries each winding's impedance correction
table onto its star leg. C ABI 7,
PowerIO IR version 2 and the IR schema `pio-ir/2/0.11.8/schema.json` are
unchanged. PowerIO.jl follows this release with the same tested binary
artifacts.

The [curated changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#0119) gives each change in full.

## What is new

### PSS/E transformer referrals

PSS/E connects a two winding transformer's magnetizing admittance from bus I
to ground, outside winding 1's ratio. The neutral branch's from-side
charging sits inside the tap, so the reader stores the admittance times the
tap squared, and bus I sees exactly `MAG1 + jMAG2` at the stated tap. That
factor is now recorded as `psse_magnetizing_referral` whenever the admittance
is nonzero. Two things follow:

- The writer divides by the recorded factor. A tap moved after reading no
  longer restates `MAG1`/`MAG2` at the new tap.
- A consumer that moves the tap holds bus I's admittance at the charging
  divided by the recorded factor.

The branch `tap` is winding 1's ratio over winding 2's, and `r + jx` already
includes winding 2's squared impedance referral. A transformer whose winding
2 ratio on its bus base is not one now records that ratio as
`psse_winding2_ratio`, so a consumer that works per winding can separate the
two again. A winding 2 at one records nothing, and fresh RAW output still
writes winding 2 at one (#799).

`Transformer3W::impedance_correction_control_winding` returns the sole
adjustable winding whose position the PSS/E `ZCOD = 1` tables follow. It is
now public, so a consumer that moves that winding can reread the tables at
its new position (#799).

### Impedance correction through the star expansion

`Transformer3W::to_star_expansion`, and so `expand_transformers_3w`, gave
each star leg its winding's tap, shift, ratings and status, but not its
impedance correction table, so correcting an expanded network left every leg
at its nominal impedance. Each leg now takes its winding's own table. Once
the native transformer is corrected, the leg also takes its nominal star
impedance, so correcting the expanded network at any controlling tap gives
the native transformer's star impedances. `ZCOD = 1` tables scale the
pairwise impedances together and stay on the native transformer: correct it
before expanding, and expand again after moving its controlling winding
(#801).

## Behavior changes

- Writing PSS/E from a network whose transformer taps were moved after
  reading now states the magnetizing admittance the source stated. Before,
  it restated it at the new tap.
- An expanded three winding transformer's star legs carry their windings'
  impedance correction tables (and, once corrected, their nominal star
  impedances) in their extras, so `apply_impedance_correction` on the
  expanded network now corrects them.
- The new extras appear only on transformers read from PSS/E RAW or RAWX.
  Converting to a format that cannot hold extras reports them like any other
  retained PSS/E field.

## Compatibility

C ABI 7 and PowerIO IR version 2 are unchanged, and IR documents keep naming
the `pio-ir/2/0.11.8/schema.json` catalog. No public item is renamed or
removed.

## Release validation

Source CI, native Python wheels and smoke tests, and paired Julia source tests
run before the release tag. The tag workflow then runs PowerIO.jl against all
five packaged C library archives before it creates a draft. Publication and
the protected registry approvals are separate steps; passing source checks
alone does not mean a package has been published.
