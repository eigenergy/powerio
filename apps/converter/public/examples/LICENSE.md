# Example files

`case9.m` is the MATPOWER case9 example, distributed under the BSD 3-Clause
license. Its source and attribution appear in the file and in
https://github.com/MATPOWER/matpower.

`feeder.dss` is a synthetic two-bus example distributed under PowerIO's
MIT OR Apache-2.0 license. It demonstrates format conversion and is not an
operational feeder model.
